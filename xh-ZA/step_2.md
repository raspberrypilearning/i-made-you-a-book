--- task ---

- 🍕 Ukufundisa umntu indlela yokwenza into ethile, njengokwenza ukutya okuthandayo
- 📖 Ukubalisa okanye ukuphinda ubalise ibali
- 🗣️ Ukwabelana ngento ngawe
- 🎨 Ukuziveza nokuyila
- Okanye enye into!
